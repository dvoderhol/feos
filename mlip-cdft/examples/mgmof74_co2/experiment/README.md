# Experimental CO2 isotherms on Mg-MOF-74 (Mg2(dobdc), CPO-27-Mg)

Downloaded 2026-11-04 from the NIST/ARPA-E Database of Novel and Emerging Adsorbent Materials (ISODB,
https://adsorption.nist.gov/isodb), adsorbents NIST-MATDB-f5e80ffaaaf05f4ed655664fd22defb9 ("Mg-MOF-74")
and NIST-MATDB-c33e43f4637310daa19ea03a823bffc6 ("Mg-DOBDC"), single-component CO2, category not "sim".
One CSV per isotherm (pressure, uptake; units in the header), `index.json` lists DOI, T, units, range.
Cite the primary DOI of every isotherm used. Uptakes at 298 K / 1 bar scatter between ~5 and ~9.6 mmol/g
(sample quality / activation); most entries do not state excess vs absolute.
